Privacy Policy

How this static website handles enquiry information

Home / Privacy Policy

Information you enter

The enquiry form prepares a message in your browser and opens WhatsApp with the details you entered. This website itself does not send the form to a server or store form submissions.

WhatsApp and external services

If you choose to send the message, WhatsApp processes it under its own terms and privacy policy. The map and web fonts may also load content from Google services.

Your choices

You can close WhatsApp without sending the message. Avoid entering sensitive personal or financial information in the enquiry form.

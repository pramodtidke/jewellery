A little care goes a long way

Keep your jewellery looking its best.

Different metals, gemstones and finishes require different care. Follow item-specific advice from your jeweller.

♧

Keep away from chemicals

As a general precaution, put jewellery on after perfume and cosmetics. Avoid contact with household cleaners.

▣

Store separately

Keep pieces in a clean, dry place and separate them where possible to reduce scratching and tangling.

◇

Clean carefully

Do not assume one cleaning method suits every gemstone or finish. Ask a jeweller before using liquids or ultrasonic cleaners.

✧

Check clasps and settings

Inspect clasps and settings periodically. Ask a jeweller to check loose stones or damaged components.

♡

Remove when appropriate

Consider removing jewellery before sports, swimming, heavy work or activities that may damage it.

✦

Ask for professional care

For valuable, delicate or unfamiliar pieces, professional advice is safer than experimenting at home.
